Signature Content Campaigns

🌿 Spring Launch

Patio Season is Coming

A multi-week countdown across Stories, Reels, and a collaborative "Patio Guide" tagging every member restaurant. Drives shares and foot traffic.

📅 March–April📱 IG + FB🎯 Reach + Saves
🏪 Weekly Series

Neighbour Spotlight

Every week, one local owner. Their story, their passion, their pick from a neighbouring shop. Members reshare — amplifying reach organically.

📅 Ongoing🎯 Engagement
🎊 Summer 2025

Taste of the Danforth Buzz

Build massive anticipation with throwbacks, community polls, and "bring it back" content. The conversation drives engagement.

📅 May–Aug🎯 Community
🗺️ Discovery Series

Hidden Gems of the Danforth

A weekly series spotlighting lesser-known shops and dishes locals love but visitors miss. Ends with "Tag a friend who needs to know." Drives discovery and new foot traffic.

📅 Weekly📱 IG + TikTok🎯 Shares
📅 Weekly Anchor Post

This Week on the Danforth

Every Monday morning, a beautifully designed carousel drops — rounding up the week's events, restaurant specials, new arrivals, and things to do. Positions the account as the #1 go-to neighbourhood guide. High saves, high return visits.

Measurement & Reporting

How We'll Measure Success

👁️
Reach
Awareness Metric
Monthly reach + impressions track how far GreekTown's message travels beyond current followers
💬
Engagement
Content Quality
Likes, saves, comments & shares per post. Saves = highest signal of valuable content on Instagram
📈
Growth
Brand Health
Follower growth rate month-over-month, plus audience quality (Toronto locals vs. bots)
🔁
Shares
Amplification
How often member businesses and community reshare — organic amplification is free reach
📍
Foot Traffic
Business Impact
Qualitative feedback from member businesses correlating social campaigns to in-store visits
📋
Monthly Report
Accountability
Full performance report to BIA team every month — transparent, data-driven, actionable insights
